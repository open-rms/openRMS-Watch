How to read this
RMS identity
A Mercury CALL carries the caller's callsign in clear, but only a 16-bit CRC of the station being called. The ACCEPT the RMS sends back carries only CRCs. Stations are keyed by that CRC.
heard
The callsign was received in clear (a CQ frame).
inferred
Exactly one known callsign has this CRC. Likely, but not proven: a CRC16 can collide.
ambiguous / unknown
Several, or no, known callsigns match the CRC.
active
The station itself transmitted (ACCEPT or CQ) within the stale window.
stale / gone
Not heard for longer than the stale window (or the gone window), while we were listening on its channels for at least that long.
unverified
Not heard recently, but we haven't listened long enough on its channels to call it stale.
called-only
Clients called this CRC but the station was never heard answering. It may be out of our receive range, or off the air.
